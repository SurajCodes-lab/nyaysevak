# Analysis: Why We're Not Ranking Yet (is it normal at 15 weeks?)

**Type:** diagnosis · **Answer: yes — normal, and even a healthy sign given the impressions.**

## The reality of a new YMYL site
- **New-domain dampening ("sandbox"):** Google deliberately holds young sites down in rank — even good ones — until they accumulate trust, history and links. Strongest in YMYL (legal), the hardest category.
- **Typical timeline:** 6–12 months for real traction; 12+ months for competitive terms. NyaySevak is at **~month 3.5**.

## Our data is a *good* sign, not a bad one
| Signal | Struggling new site | NyaySevak |
|--------|--------------------|-----------|
| Indexed? | no/partial | ✅ yes (clean coverage) |
| Impressions | ~0 | ✅ ~5,800 / quarter |
| Position | not shown | ~20 (page 2–3) |

Getting ~5,800 impressions at position ~20 means **Google likes the content and is holding rank until trust builds** — the profile of a site that *will* rank. A failing new site is *not indexed* or gets *zero* impressions; neither applies.

## Why rank hasn't come (in order)
1. **Age/trust** — 15 weeks vs competitors' 10–16 years. Only time + authority fix it.
2. **Zero backlinks** vs their thousands — the accelerant not yet added.
3. **The new pages are days old** — Google hasn't fully crawled/evaluated them; new pages take weeks–months to settle.

## Normal vs problem
- **Normal (us):** indexed, impressions rising, pos 20–50, few clicks, new pages not ranked yet. ✅
- **A problem (not us):** not indexed, 0 impressions, coverage errors, manual penalty. ❌

## The honest part
"Normal for 15 weeks" ≠ "it fixes itself." Sites that break out at 6–9 months are the ones that **built authority in the meantime**. Without off-page (GBP + reviews, citations, backlinks) the trajectory stalls at position 20; with it, you accelerate past normal. That is the single lever every report points to.
