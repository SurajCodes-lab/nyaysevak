# Analysis: Competitor Gap (where we lack, where we can win)

**Type:** research / diagnosis · **Sources:** Similarweb, Semrush, live page inspection (LawRato).

## The field
| Platform | Scale / age | Model |
|----------|-------------|-------|
| Vakilsearch | 5,000+ advocates, since 2010, funded | directory + instant paid consult + registrations |
| LawRato | 10,000+ advocates, 700+ cities, huge Q&A | directory + session consult |
| LegalKart | ~2,500 advocates, app, subscription | instant call + 30-day follow-up |
| IndiaFilings | #9 Business Services (India) | productised registrations/compliance |
| **NyaySevak** | **~15 weeks, ~0 backlinks** | platform matching + free assessment |

## The hard numbers
- **Vakilsearch: ~9,040 referring domains, ~1.24M backlinks, ~1M monthly visits, ~30% direct (brand) traffic.**
- **NyaySevak: ~0 referring domains.**

## Where we lack (ranked)
1. **Authority & age** — 0 backlinks vs thousands; 15 weeks vs 10–16 years. Decisive.
2. **No instant consultation** — they connect in minutes; we promise a callback.
3. **No visible reviews/ratings** — theirs show ⭐ stars in Google; ours don't.
4. **No advocate directory** — LawRato's 10,000 profiles are its SEO moat; our BCI stance forbids this.
5. **Content library scale** — 32 guides + 57 glossary vs their thousands of Q&As.
6. **Weak follow-up** and **unknown brand + name-twin (`nyayasevak.com`)**.

## Where we can win (don't fight head-on)
- **Niche depth** on the 13 focus matters beats a generalist for a founder choosing an NCLT/IP lawyer.
- **Technical SEO / AEO / GEO** — schema, `llms.txt`, Quick Answers; competitors lack these → faster AI-citation.
- **Winnable informational long-tail** where we already rank pos 5–10.
- **Premium, compliant brand** — "we match you, we don't advertise lawyers" reads as trustworthy.

## Takeaway
We cannot out-scale them. We can own a niche via winnable SEO + AEO, build off-page authority (the real ceiling), and close two operational gaps: faster response and real reviews (GBP).
