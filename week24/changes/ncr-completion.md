# Change: NCR Completion — Ghaziabad Parity + Greater Noida

**Status:** ✅ Shipped · **Commit:** `4838cc8` · **Type:** content (local intent)

## What was wrong
NCR was already the best-covered region on the site after Weeks 18–19, but it had two holes. **Ghaziabad** carried only 2 of 6 matter pages (cheque bounce and RERA) while Delhi, Noida, Gurgaon and Faridabad all carried 6. And **Greater Noida** — the NCR's largest planned-development belt, and the epicentre of GNIDA/YEIDA allotment disputes and stalled builder-buyer projects — had no page at all.

## What we changed
- **Ghaziabad +4 matter pages** → 6/6: anticipatory bail, mutual-consent divorce, company registration, NCLT/IBC.
- **Greater Noida added** as a city with a hub and all 5 core practice verticals.

Greater Noida is deliberately differentiated from Noida by the **allotting authority (GNIDA / YEIDA), not the courthouse** — both cities are heard at the same Gautam Buddh Nagar complex at Surajpur. The pages say this explicitly rather than implying a separate court, which is both accurate and the honest way to avoid cannibalising the Noida pages.

Both cities' corporate and NCLT pages state the fact that catches out most NCR businesses: a company registered in Uttar Pradesh files at the **NCLT Allahabad Bench**, not New Delhi, regardless of where it trades.

## Why it helps
- Completes the region where the site already has the most authority, so these pages should rank faster than the Gujarat/Rajasthan additions.
- Greater Noida's RERA and allotment disputes are high-value, high-intent matters with a large affected population.
- Ghaziabad's industrial belt (Sahibabad, Site IV, Tronica City) drives exactly the cheque-bounce, recovery and company-registration volume the new matter pages target.
