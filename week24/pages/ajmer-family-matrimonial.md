**Covers:** an unusually high proportion of government and railway employees among local litigants, which makes maintenance **both easier to quantify and easier to enforce** — income is documented from the pay slip and service record, and arrears can be recovered by **attachment of salary** through the employer rather than ordinary execution. Pension, gratuity, provident fund, nomination and family-pension entitlements therefore feature more than elsewhere and must be addressed expressly, or the settlement generates fresh litigation at retirement.
