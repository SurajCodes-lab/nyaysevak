## Target queries / why it matters
**The most important omission in the previous city set.** Jodhpur is the *principal seat* of the Rajasthan High Court — Jaipur is only a bench — so writs and appeals from the western districts (Barmer, Jaisalmer, Bikaner, Pali, Jalore, Sirohi, Nagaur) are filed here. The pages also state where the map diverges: Jodhpur's High Court work is at Jodhpur, but its **NCLT work is at Jaipur**.
