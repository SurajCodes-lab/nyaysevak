**Covers:** two mechanisms outsiders miss — Gujarat's **Town Planning Scheme** (land pooled, part reserved, a reconstituted *final plot* returned with a betterment charge; disputes go to the Town Planning Officer, not a civil court) and **new-tenure land**, which cannot be transferred without prior permission and premium, making an uninformed purchase one of the costliest errors in the state. Plus GujRERA remedies and the Gujarat Ownership Flats Act for pre-RERA buildings. **Case types:** title & tenure diligence, GujRERA complaints, TP Scheme disputes, new-tenure/agricultural land, society & redevelopment, market-shop transactions, specific performance, partition.
